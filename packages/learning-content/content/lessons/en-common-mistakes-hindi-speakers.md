Many phrases that are common in India are not standard English. They are understood by people around you, but they can confuse colleagues in other countries and they can cost you marks in an interview. This lesson lists the mistakes that appear most often at work, with the natural alternative for each. You do not need to fix everything. Pick three, use them for a week, then pick three more.

> **Hinglish mein:** Yeh list "tum galat ho" batane ke liye nahi hai. Yeh woh phrases hain jo hum Hindi se translate karke bolte hain. Bas sahi version ek hafte tak jaan-boojh kar use karo, phir aadat ban jaati hai.

## Words that mean something different

| Common phrase | Say this instead | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I have a doubt. | I have a question. / I'm not sure about this part. | In English, "doubt" means you do not believe something. A question is what you actually have. |
| Please prepone the meeting. | Please move the meeting earlier. | "Prepone" is not used outside India. |
| I will revert back to you. | I will get back to you. / I will reply by evening. | In tech, "revert" means undo, like `git revert`. Say "reply" or "get back to you". |
| Please do the needful. | Please send the report by Friday. | "The needful" is vague. Say exactly what you need. |
| Kindly do this. | Could you please do this? | "Kindly" sounds old-fashioned or commanding. |
| What is your good name? | What is your name? | "Good name" is a direct translation of "shubh naam". |
| I passed out of college in 2022. | I graduated in 2022. | "Passed out" can mean fainting. |

## Extra words you do not need

| Common phrase | Say this instead |
| --- | --- |
| Return back | Return (or come back) |
| Discuss about the plan | Discuss the plan |
| Cope up with pressure | Cope with pressure |
| More better | Better |
| Enter into the room | Enter the room |
| Explain me the code | Explain the code to me |
| I am agree | I agree |

## Uncountable words

These words have no plural form and do not take "a". Use **some** or a unit like "a piece of".

| Wrong | Correct |
| --- | --- |
| informations | information |
| advices | advice |
| feedbacks | feedback |
| a staff | a member of staff / the staff |
| knowledges | knowledge |
| equipments | equipment |

Example: "Thanks for the feedback. I need more information about the error."

## Time and tense mistakes

| Common phrase | Say this instead | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I am working here since 2022. | I have been working here since 2022. | For a period that started in the past and continues, use the present perfect. |
| I am working here since three years. | I have been working here for three years. | Use **for** with a length of time and **since** with a starting point. |
| Today morning, yesterday night | This morning, last night | English uses "this" and "last" with these words. |
| I am knowing the answer. | I know the answer. | Know, understand, want and like are not used with -ing. |
| He is having a laptop. | He has a laptop. | Have for owning things does not take -ing. |

## Introductions

| Common phrase | Say this instead |
| --- | --- |
| Myself Rahul. | I am Rahul. / My name is Rahul. |
| I have 3 years experience. | I have three years of experience. |
| I am from Pune only. | I am from Pune. |
| You are coming, isn't it? | You are coming, aren't you? / You are coming, right? |

## Say them aloud

```phrases
I have a question about this ticket.
I will get back to you by this evening.
Could you move the meeting a little earlier?
Thanks for the feedback. It really helps.
I have been working on this service since January.
This morning I fixed the login bug.
```

## Practice

1. Choose three mistakes from the tables above that you make most often. Write them on a sticky note.
2. For one week, check your messages and emails for those three phrases before you press send.
3. Rewrite these in natural English: "I have one doubt in this code." "I will revert back tomorrow." "Please discuss about the issue." "I am working here since 2021."
4. Check: "I have a question about this code." "I will get back to you tomorrow." "Please discuss the issue." "I have been working here since 2021."

## Recap

- Replace doubt with question, prepone with move earlier, revert back with get back to you.
- Do not add extra words: return back, discuss about, cope up.
- Information, advice and feedback are never plural.
- Use for with a duration and since with a start point, with the present perfect.
- Fix three mistakes a week. Slow and steady is faster than a big list you forget.

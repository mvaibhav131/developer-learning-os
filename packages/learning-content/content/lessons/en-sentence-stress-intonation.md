Word stress is about one word. **Sentence stress** is about the whole sentence. English speakers make some words strong and clear, and squeeze the rest. This gives English its rhythm, and it shows the listener which words are important. When you learn sentence stress, you become easier to understand, and it also becomes easier to understand fast native speakers.

> **Hinglish mein:** English mein har word ko barabar zor se nahi bolte. Jo word important hai (noun, main verb, adjective) usse zor dete hain. Chhote words (a, the, to, of, can) halke aur tez bolte hain.

## Content words and function words

| Type | Examples | How you say them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Content words**: nouns, main verbs, adjectives, adverbs, question words, negatives | bug, fixed, slow, quickly, why, not | Strong, longer and clearer |
| **Function words**: articles, prepositions, auxiliary verbs, pronouns | a, the, to, of, can, was, he | Short and weak |

See how the stress falls on the important words (capital letters):

| Written | Stress pattern |
| --- | --- |
| I fixed the bug in the login module. | I FIXED the BUG in the LOGin MODule. |
| Did you push the changes? | Did you PUSH the CHANGes? |

## Weak forms

Function words often change their sound when they are not stressed.

| Word | Weak sound | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| to | "tuh" | I want to fix it. |
| and | "un" | tests and docs |
| can | "kun" | I can help. |
| for | "fuh" | wait for me |
| of | "uv" | a lot of bugs |
| a | "uh" | a new feature |

Be careful: **can't** is strong and clear, but "can" is weak. That is why people stress the negative: "I CAN'T make it." If you say "I can make it" with a weak "can", people hear a yes.

## Intonation: the music of the voice

| Situation | Voice | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Statements | Falls at the end | We deployed on Friday. (falling) |
| Wh- questions | Falls at the end | Where is the file? (falling) |
| Yes or no questions | Rises at the end | Is the build green? (rising) |
| A list | Rises on each item, falls on the last | We use Java, Python, and Go. |
| Checking or uncertainty | Rises | We release on Friday? |

A flat voice sounds bored or unsure. Use more movement in your voice, especially in meetings.

## Changing the stress changes the meaning

| Sentence | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **I** didn't write the test. | Somebody else did. |
| I **didn't** write the test. | I did not write it. |
| I didn't write the **test**. | I wrote something else. |

## Say them aloud

```phrases
I fixed the bug in the login module.
Did you push the changes this morning?
We use Java, Python and Go.
I can't join the call at three.
The build failed because the test was slow.
Could you explain this part again?
```

## Practice

1. Pick a sentence from your stand-up. Underline the content words.
2. Say it slowly with a clear stress on the underlined words and weak function words.
3. Say it again at normal speed. Keep the same rhythm. Do not say every word with equal strength.
4. Clap or tap the table on each stressed word. The time between taps should be about the same.
5. Record yourself and compare with a clip from a video.

## Recap

- Stress content words. Weaken function words.
- Can is weak, but can't is strong.
- Statements and wh- questions fall. Yes or no questions rise.
- Changing the stress changes the meaning.
- Rhythm improves with listening and shadowing, not only with rules.

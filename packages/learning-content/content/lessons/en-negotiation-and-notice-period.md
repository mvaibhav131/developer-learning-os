Salary and notice period are some of the most stressful topics for many candidates. The English is simple. The difficulty is the confidence to say it. A calm, polite, honest conversation helps you much more than a long argument. You are not asking for a favour. You are agreeing on a fair exchange of work and pay. This lesson gives you a safe structure and phrases. It does not tell you to hide the truth, and you should never lie about your current salary or about other offers.

> **Hinglish mein:** Salary ki baat shaanti se aur honestly karo. Pehle market research karo, phir ek range batao jo tumhare skills ke hisaab se sahi ho. Jhooth mat bolo: na current salary ke baare mein, na doosre offer ke baare mein.

## Before the conversation

1. **Research:** look up the typical range for your role, city and experience, using job sites and people you trust.
2. **Know your number:** decide the minimum you would accept and a range you consider fair.
3. **Think beyond salary:** joining bonus, learning budget, remote days, review cycle, health insurance.
4. **Know your notice period:** how many days, and whether your company can reduce it. Check your contract.

## Talking about salary

| Situation | Say |
| --- | --- |
| The recruiter asks your expectation | Based on my experience and the market for this role, I'm looking for a range of X to Y. |
| You want them to speak first | Could you share the budget range for this role? |
| They ask your current salary | My current compensation is X. I'm looking for a role that reflects my growth and the market range. |
| You want to negotiate | Thank you for the offer. Is there any flexibility on the base salary? |
| You need time | Thank you, I'm excited about the offer. Could I have a few days to review it? |
| You accept | Thank you. I'm happy to accept the offer. |

Always give a **range** and a **reason** (skills, experience, market), not only a number.

## Talking about your notice period

| Situation | Say |
| --- | --- |
| They ask about your notice period | My notice period is 60 days. |
| It can be shortened | It may be possible to reduce it, and I will discuss it with my manager. |
| You can join earlier | I can join within 30 days if my current company agrees. |
| It is fixed | It is fixed at 90 days, but I can finish my handover early. |

Tell the truth and do not promise something that depends on another person.

## A sample conversation

> **Recruiter:** What are your salary expectations?
> **You:** Based on my three years of experience and the market for backend developers in this city, I'm looking for a range of X to Y. I'm open to discussing the full package.
> **Recruiter:** The budget is slightly lower than that.
> **You:** I understand. Is there any flexibility, for example in the base salary or a review after six months?
> **Recruiter:** We can review after six months.
> **You:** That sounds fair. Could I have two days to think about it? Thank you for the offer.

Replace X and Y with your own researched numbers.

## Useful phrases

```phrases
Based on my experience and the market, I'm looking for a range of X to Y.
Could you share the budget range for this role?
Thank you for the offer. Is there any flexibility on the base salary?
My notice period is sixty days.
Could I have a few days to review the offer?
I'm happy to accept the offer. Thank you.
```

## What not to say

| Avoid | Why | Better |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whatever you give is fine. | You lose negotiating power. | I'm looking for a range of X to Y. |
| I need 50 percent more. | No reason is given. | Based on my skills and the market, I'm looking for X. |
| I have another offer. (but you do not) | It is a lie. | I'm also speaking with other companies. (only if true) |
| I will think and call you. (and you never call) | It is unprofessional. | I will reply by Friday. |

## Practice

1. Write your own sentence for "What are your salary expectations?" with a range and a reason.
2. Write your own answer for "What is your notice period?"
3. Say both aloud three times in a calm voice, and record them.
4. Prepare one polite negotiation sentence and one sentence to ask for time.

## Recap

- Research the range. Give a range with a reason.
- Be honest about your current salary, your notice period and other offers.
- Ask about flexibility and the full package.
- Take a day or two to decide, and reply when you said you would.

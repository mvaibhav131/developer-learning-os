Shadowing means you listen to a speaker and repeat what they say almost at the same time, like a shadow. You copy the words, the rhythm, the speed and the music of the voice. It is one of the simplest ways to train your mouth to move in English, and it needs no partner. It will not make you fluent overnight, but a short daily practice builds the habit of speaking out loud, which is where fluency really comes from.

> **Hinglish mein:** Shadowing = kisi speaker ko sunte hue uske peeche-peeche bolna, jaise parchhai. Isse tumhara muh English ki speed aur rhythm ka aadi ho jata hai. Roz 10-15 minute kaafi hai.

## Why it helps

- It trains **pronunciation, stress and rhythm** together.
- It trains your **mouth muscles** for English sounds.
- It trains you to **speak without translating**, because there is no time.
- It teaches useful chunks of real English, such as "Let me check", "I'll get back to you".

## What to use

Choose a clip that is **one to two minutes long**, spoken clearly, with a **transcript** if possible. Good sources are the videos in this lesson, a developer stand-up video, or a short interview. Use slower speed (0.75x in the video settings) at the start.

## Five-day plan for one clip

| Day | What to do | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Listen to the clip two times without the text. What is the main idea? | 10 min |
| 2 | Read the transcript. Look up new words. Read it aloud with the audio. | 15 min |
| 3 | Shadow with the text. Pause the video after each sentence and repeat. | 15 min |
| 4 | Shadow without the text, without pausing. Keep up with the speaker. | 15 min |
| 5 | Record yourself speaking the full clip. Listen and compare. Pick two things to fix. | 15 min |

After five days, choose a new clip. Come back to old clips after a week.

## Do and don't

| Do | Do not |
| --- | --- |
| Start with a slow, clear speaker | Pick a very fast speaker because it is "advanced" |
| Use headphones and a quiet place | Whisper. Use your real speaking voice |
| Copy the music and stress, not only the words | Stop at every difficult word |
| Practice every day, even for 10 minutes | Practice for two hours once a week |
| Keep the same clip for five days | Change the clip every day |

## Shadowing for work

Choose material from your own world: a stand-up meeting, a tech talk, a code review discussion. You will meet the same phrases again at work, which makes them stick.

```phrases
Good morning, everyone. Let's start with a quick update.
Yesterday I finished the login feature and opened a pull request.
Today I'm going to work on the payment tests.
I'm blocked on the API key, so I need some help.
Thanks, that's all from my side.
```

## Honest expectations

- Shadowing helps, but results depend on regular practice. Nobody can promise a fixed number of days.
- It will feel awkward at first. That is normal.
- Mix it with other practice: speaking, writing and listening.

## Recap

- Shadowing means repeating after a speaker almost at the same time.
- Use short clear clips with transcripts, and keep the same clip for five days.
- Copy rhythm and stress, not only words.
- Ten to fifteen minutes daily is better than a long session once a week.
